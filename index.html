Core Features

Built for institutions.
Invisible to end users.

⚡

Atomic Micro-Packet Slicing

Every transaction splits into 10 independent Stellar payments. Zero single points of failure. Each packet produces a real, verifiable on-chain hash — not a simulation.

🛡️

Sovereign Shield

Real-time DEX order book monitoring enforces a strict 1 basis point slippage ceiling. Breach triggers Symphony HITL escalation directly to the client's compliance officer.

🔐

Non-Custodial by Architecture

CenDTrus never holds client funds. Private keys exist in memory only for the duration of one transaction signing operation. We are the Pipe, not the Water.

🧠

Symphony HITL Compliance

When the Shield triggers, the decision escalates to the client's own compliance officer — not to us. Full detail, slippage reading, and risk context surfaced for human review.

⬡

Qubic Quorum Audit

Completed settlement bundles receive external cryptographic attestation from the Qubic 676-Computor network — an independent audit layer beyond Stellar's native finality.

🔗

On-Chain Verifiability

Every packet hash is publicly verifiable on Stellar Expert in real time. No black boxes. No custodian. Cryptographic proof on every peso that moves through the rail.

Sovereign Shield

Compliance without
giving up control.

The Sovereign Shield monitors the Stellar DEX in real time. When it triggers, the decision belongs to your compliance officer — not to us.

Ceiling: 0.010% (1 basis point)
Live DEX Monitor
SECURE
Slippage reading well inside the 1bp ceiling. Sovereign Shield clears the transaction. All 10 packets fire automatically. No intervention required.
WARN
Slippage approaching the ceiling (≥0.75bp). Monitoring frequency doubles. HITL placed on standby. Settlement proceeds but is tracked at 500ms intervals.
BREACH — HITL TRIGGERED
Slippage exceeds 1bp. Pipeline halts instantly. Symphony HITL escalates to the client's own compliance officer — full transaction detail, slippage reading, and risk context surfaced. Their call. Not ours.
